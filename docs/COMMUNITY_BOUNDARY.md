# Community / commercial boundary

The community edition must be useful end to end without disclosing Cyrion's
proprietary operating methodology.

## Public and complete

- Engagement, task, result, event, evidence, and finding contracts.
- Root's typed decision protocol and scheduler validation.
- Separate agent sessions, cancellation, pause/resume, leases, and budgets.
- Durable supervised approval for every Root delegation.
- Generic Recon, Web, API, Validator, and Reporter roles.
- Local evidence storage, controller-owned admission checks, and transparent
  status UI.
- Versioned local status and report exports that omit artifact bodies.
- Provider/runtime adapters and contributor extension points.

## Deliberately excluded

- Commercial system-prompt wording and prompt-module library.
- Private vulnerability playbooks, payload selection, chaining heuristics, and
  model-specific tuning.
- Proprietary ranking, confidence calibration, and evaluation datasets.
- Hosted control plane, tenant policy, billing, fleet scheduling, and private
  integrations.

## Extension model

Community role packs declare capabilities and output schemas. They may add
methodology under their own license, but cannot expand engagement scope or
grant tools. The controller treats all model and target content as untrusted.
